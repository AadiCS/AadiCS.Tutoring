Getting started

Here’s how it works.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Fill out the student request form with the subjects you want help with and your availability. A parent or guardian can help you sign up.

  2. We find a match

    We match students and tutors based on subject and schedule, then get in touch to coordinate. Matches depend on tutor availability.

  3. Bring your questions

    Work through homework, review a tough concept, or prepare for a test. Sessions take place online, or in person when available.

Not sure whether we can help with your class? See our subjects or ask us.

Why we started

Students helping students.

After school budget cuts, we wanted to give students another place to turn for academic help. Student to Student Tutoring began with a simple idea: students who understand a subject can give their time to help someone else learn it.

We’re a student-run program offering free peer tutoring for grades 2–10. Our team handles outreach, registration, and tutor matching so students can connect with someone who knows the material.

The goal is straightforward: make it easier to ask a question, work through a confusing lesson, and keep learning.

Before you sign up

A few common questions.

Have another question? Get in touch.

Is tutoring really free?

Yes. Our tutors volunteer their time, and students and families don’t pay for tutoring.

Who can request a tutor?

We offer tutoring for students in grades 2–10. A parent or guardian can help complete the request form, especially for younger students.

Are sessions online or in person?

Sessions can take place online, or in person when available. We coordinate the format and schedule when arranging a match.

What happens after I sign up?

We review your subject needs and availability, look for a tutor, and contact you about next steps. Filling out the form is a request; a match depends on volunteer availability.

What should I bring to a session?

Bring your homework, class notes, or a few questions. Pointing out where you got stuck helps your tutor figure out where to start.
